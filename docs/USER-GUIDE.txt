palmkobo - Palm OS on your Kobo
===============================

palmkobo runs Palm OS 4.1 (an emulated Palm m500) on a Kobo e-reader, using
the CloudpilotEmu emulator. You get the Palm screen and Graffiti area on the
e-ink display, the Palm's hardware buttons along the bottom, Bluetooth
keyboard support, a virtual SD card you can fill and empty over USB, and
your Palm's state is kept between sessions.

STATUS: experimental. Developed and tested on a Kobo Clara BW. Other Kobo
models that FBInk and NickelMenu support should work, but are untested -
please report how it goes on yours.


WHAT YOU NEED
-------------

  * A Kobo with NickelMenu installed (https://pgaskin.net/NickelMenu/).
  * A Palm m500 ROM image, dumped from your own Palm m500.
    No ROMs are included - Palm OS ROMs are copyrighted.
    Tip: check that your ROM boots in CloudpilotEmu's web version
    (https://cloudpilot-emu.github.io/app) before copying it to the Kobo.
  * A computer to copy files to the Kobo over USB.


INSTALLING
----------

1. Connect the Kobo to your computer and tap "Connect" on the Kobo.

2. Make hidden files visible. Kobo folders start with a dot (.adds, .kobo).
   Windows: File Explorer > View > Show > Hidden items
            (also turn on "File name extensions").
   macOS:   press Cmd+Shift+. in Finder.

3. Open the KOBO folder in this package and copy what is inside it (the
   .adds folder) to the root of the Kobo's drive. If you already have an
   .adds folder, merge them - nothing of yours is replaced.

   You should now have:
     .adds/palmkobo/   (palmkobo itself)
     .adds/nm/palmkobo (the NickelMenu entries)

4. Copy your ROM into .adds/palmkobo and rename it to exactly
       m500.rom
   (With file name extensions visible, make sure it is not m500.rom.bin.)

5. Eject the Kobo. NickelMenu now has "PalmOS" and "PalmOS input test".

6. Run "PalmOS input test" first. Tap near each corner of the screen: the
   cross must appear under your finger. Tap EXIT in the middle of the
   screen, or press the Kobo's power button, to leave the test. (If the cross is in the wrong place, see TROUBLESHOOTING.)

7. Run "PalmOS". The first start boots the Palm from the ROM and takes you
   through Palm OS setup (pen calibration etc.).


USING IT
--------

The screen shows the Palm display, the Graffiti/silkscreen area below it,
and a row of buttons:

  DATE ADDR TODO MEMO   the Palm's four application buttons
  ^  v                  the Palm's scroll rocker
  RFSH                  full screen refresh (clears e-ink ghosting)
  PWR                   the Palm's power button
  EXIT                  tap twice to save and return to the Kobo

Write Graffiti in the box as you would with a stylus; your finger's trail is
shown while you write. Tap the silkscreen icons (Home, Menu, Calc, Find)
as usual.

Leaving: tap EXIT twice, or press the Kobo's power button. Your Palm is saved
and the Kobo's own screen comes back. The next launch continues exactly where
you left off.

Autosave: while you use it, the Palm (and SD card) is saved at most every
5 minutes, during a short pause in your typing or tapping.

Idle exit: after 15 minutes without input, palmkobo saves and returns to the
Kobo so it can sleep. A warning appears a minute before - tap the screen or
press a key to stay.

Note: the Kobo itself does not go to sleep while PalmOS is running.


KEYBOARD (BLUETOOTH OR USB)
---------------------------

Connect the keyboard in the Kobo's own Bluetooth settings BEFORE starting
PalmOS. "PalmOS input test" shows the keys it receives, so you can check it.

  letters, numbers, punctuation (US layout)  typed into the Palm
  Enter, Backspace, Tab, arrow keys          same on the Palm
  F1 F2 F3 F4                                Date Book, Address, To Do, Memo
  Page Up / Page Down                        scroll up / down
  Esc (or F5)                                Palm Menu
  F6 (or Home)                               Applications launcher
  F7                                         Find
  F8                                         Calculator
  F9                                         full screen refresh

Ctrl/Alt shortcuts are not passed to the Palm; use Esc for menus.
Page-turn remotes (Page Up/Down only) also work, for scrolling.


INSTALLING PALM APPS
--------------------

Two ways, both by copying files to the Kobo over USB:

  .adds/palmkobo/install/   .prc/.pdb files here are installed into the
                            Palm's main memory on the next launch (wait a
                            few seconds after start). Installed files are
                            moved to install/installed.

  .adds/palmkobo/to-card/   files here are copied onto the virtual SD card
                            on the next launch (see below). Apps put here
                            show up in the launcher's card category.


THE SD CARD
-----------

The Palm has a virtual SD card (.adds/palmkobo/card.img, 64 MB), created
automatically on first launch.

  Into the Palm:  copy files into .adds/palmkobo/to-card/ and start PalmOS.
                  .prc/.pdb/.pqa files go to /PALM/Launcher (so apps appear
                  in the launcher's card category); other files and folders
                  keep their layout. Imported files move to to-card/imported.

  Out of the Palm: after every exit, .adds/palmkobo/card-contents/ holds an
                  exact copy of everything on the card. Copy files from there.
                  Don't edit that folder - it is rewritten on every exit.

In the Palm launcher, choose the card's category (top-right list) to see
apps on the card. App > Copy... in the launcher menu copies apps between the
Palm and the card. Apps that save plain text files to the card (VFS-aware
editors) give you files you can open directly on your computer.

To start with an empty card, delete card.img.


GRAYSCALE
---------

Palm OS and most apps on the m500 draw in black and white. Apps written for
grayscale Palms (for example the grayscale build of Space Trader) show 16
shades of gray. While your finger is on the screen the display uses a fast
black-and-white mode; grays return a moment after you lift it.


A SECOND PALM: m515 (OPTIONAL)
------------------------------

If you also have a ROM dumped from a Palm m515 (colour, shown in grays):
  1. Copy it to .adds/palmkobo/m515.rom
  2. Open .adds/nm/palmkobo in a text editor and remove the # in front of the
     "PalmOS m515" line.
  3. Eject (restart the Kobo if the menu entry doesn't appear).
The m515 keeps its own saved state (session-m515.img) and shares the SD card.


SETTINGS
--------

.adds/palmkobo/palmkobo.ini can be edited with any text editor. Each setting
is explained in the file. The most useful:

  scale              screen size (0 = largest that fits)
  touch_swap_axes,   fix touch orientation (auto, 0 or 1)
  touch_mirror_x/y
  graffiti_echo      show your finger's trail in the Graffiti box
  full_refresh_every how often to do a flashing refresh to clear ghosting
  autosave_minutes   0 turns autosave off
  idle_exit_minutes  0 turns idle exit off
  card_size_mb       size of a newly created SD card (4 - 128)
  hotsync_name       your Palm user name


OPTIONAL: SKIP THE "CONNECT" PROMPT
-----------------------------------

To make the Kobo connect to your computer as soon as it's plugged in, add
this to .kobo/Kobo/Kobo eReader.conf:

  [DeveloperSettings]
  AutoUsbGadget=true

(Downside: you can't read while it charges from a computer.) While PalmOS is
running, the Kobo won't connect over USB at all - exit PalmOS first.


TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------

Everything palmkobo does is logged in .adds/palmkobo/palmkobo.log.

"ROM NOT FOUND" / "ROM NOT SUPPORTED" on screen
    The ROM must be .adds/palmkobo/m500.rom and be a Palm m500 ROM. Check it
    in CloudpilotEmu's web version.

Taps land in the wrong place
    Run "PalmOS input test", note where the cross appears, and set
    touch_swap_axes / touch_mirror_x / touch_mirror_y in palmkobo.ini to 0 or
    1 (instead of auto) until the cross follows your finger.

Keyboard does nothing
    Connect it in the Kobo's Bluetooth settings first, then start PalmOS.
    Check "PalmOS input test", and look for "keyboard connected" in the log.

The screen looks smeary or ghosted
    Tap RFSH (or press F9).

Start the Palm from scratch
    Delete .adds/palmkobo/session.img (your Palm's saved state).

Something is stuck
    Hold the Kobo's power button to restart it. Your books and the Kobo are
    not affected; the Palm is restored from its last save.


UNINSTALLING
------------

Delete the folder .adds/palmkobo and the file .adds/nm/palmkobo, then eject.
(This deletes your Palm's saved state and SD card - copy session.img and
card.img somewhere first if you want to keep them.)
